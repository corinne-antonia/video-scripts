Growth Video Scripts

What the research says

The format we are copying

0:00–0:05Hook

Name the viewer's problem or a surprising claim in one sentence. No intro, no "hey guys."

0:05–0:45Retain

Three tight points or steps. Every line moves forward. Cut anything that doesn't.

0:45–1:00Reward

Pay off the hook's promise with one clear action, then a quiet CTA.

One idea per video

B2B short-form that performs is a single practical insight in 30–90 seconds. The educational "tip clip" is the workhorse.

Promise, then keep it

Hormozi's rule: if the hook promises three reasons, deliver three. Retention dies when the payoff doesn't match.

Problem → better way

Open on a pain the buyer already feels, then show the better approach. This beats feature talk every time.

Test hooks, not videos

Film 2–3 hooks per script. Same body, different first line. Keep the one that holds viewers.

Filming setup

How to shoot these

  • Look: one speaker, direct to camera, chest-up, plain background, solid-colour top. Same setup every time so the series reads as one brand.
  • Edit: jump cuts every 2–4 seconds, bold captions on screen the whole time, no music under the hook. On-screen text cues are listed under each beat.
  • Batch: film 6 scripts in one session, plus the alternate hooks. That's 3 weeks of content at 2 posts a week.
  • Channels: LinkedIn and X first (that's where founders and BD leads are), then YouTube Shorts and Reels.
  • CTA: keep it soft and consistent, e.g. "Follow for one growth fix a week" or "Comment AUDIT and I'll look at your funnel."
  • Pace: each script is ~140–160 spoken words. Read at a calm, confident speed and you'll land near 60 seconds.
